96

DIRECTOR: C. Prem Kumar

REVIEW:

96 is a deeply emotional Tamil romantic drama directed by C. Prem Kumar and starring Vijay Sethupathi and Trisha Krishnan. The film beautifully explores nostalgia, lost love, and the pain of missed opportunities through the reunion of two former school lovers after many years. Instead of relying on heavy drama or twists, the movie focuses on subtle emotions, meaningful conversations, and realistic performances, which made audiences connect strongly with the characters. Vijay Sethupathi’s restrained acting and Trisha’s graceful screen presence are major highlights, while Govind Vasantha’s soulful music adds emotional depth, especially in songs like Kaathalae Kaathalae. The story follows K. Ramachandran, commonly known as Ram, a travel photographer who leads a quiet and lonely life. During a visit to his hometown, he reconnects with his school friends and attends a reunion after more than twenty years. There, he meets Janaki Devi, known as Jaanu, the girl he loved deeply during his school days. Although both have moved on with their lives, the reunion brings back countless memories and emotions that they had buried over the years. As Ram and Jaanu spend time together reminiscing about the past, they reflect on their love, regrets, choices, and the paths their lives have taken.
